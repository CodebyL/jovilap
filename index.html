Questions

Before you call

What is the free draft?

A first look at how your website could look, based on what you tell us about your business. There's no cost and no commitment.

Whose name is the website under?

Your business's. The web address is registered to you from day one, and the finished site is yours after the final payment.

How soon can it launch?

That mostly depends on how quickly your photos and details come in. Your written quote includes a target launch date.

Is the $99 care plan required?

No. It covers hosting, backups and small edits each month. Without it, you arrange hosting and changes yourself.

Can I cancel care later?

Yes. After the first six months you can stop in any month, and JOVILAP helps move the site wherever you choose.

Will this put me at the top of Google?

Nobody can honestly promise that. A clear, fast site helps the people who do find you decide to call.

Who writes the words on the site?

You fill out a short questionnaire about your business. JOVILAP turns your answers into clear website copy.
