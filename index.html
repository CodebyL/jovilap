    Six steps from first conversation to launch. You always know what's next and what you owe.

      Optional monthly care keeps your site online and handles small updates.

    Questions

    Common questions

    Is the website review really free?

    Yes. It's a 20–30 minute conversation with no obligation. You'll get a written price afterward if you want to move forward.

    Do I own my website?

    Yes. Once you've paid in full, the website and its content are yours. Your domain name is registered in your own name from the start.

    How long does it take?

    It depends mostly on how quickly your photos and information arrive. You'll get an estimated launch date once JOVILAP has your materials.

    Do I have to sign up for the care plan?

    No, it's optional. Without it, you'll need to arrange your own hosting and updates. With it, hosting, backups, and small changes are handled for you.

    What if I stop the care plan?

    After the 6-month minimum you can stop any month. You keep your domain and content, and JOVILAP will help move your website to your own hosting.

    Will my website get me more customers or rank first on Google?

    No one can honestly guarantee that. A clear website makes it easier for customers who find you to trust you and contact you. Search ranking depends on many things outside the website.

    Do you write the text for my website?

    You answer a short questionnaire and JOVILAP turns your answers into clear website text. Writing everything from scratch without your input is not included.
