How a visit works

You See the Problem Before You Pay

Four steps, the same for a squeak or a big repair.

  1. 01

    Tell us what's happening

    A noise, a light on the dash, a pull to one side. Call or send the form.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis first

    The car gets checked properly before anyone talks about parts.

  3. 03

    We show you

    You see what's worn or broken and hear what it costs to fix.

  4. 04

    You decide

    Only the work you approve gets done. Then you're back on the road.

No surprises on the bill: the diagnosis comes first, then a clear explanation, then your yes or no.
